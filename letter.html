♡ To my almost birthday twin Tap the envelope to open your letter Tap again to fold your letter away

Happy 23rd birthday, Ate Carmel Eunice!

Whenever we'd see each other in every other vacation we can, I felt so happy, shy, scared, and sad, basically a ton of conflicting emotions. You were the closest thing I had to an older sister, a sibling in which I've always wanted to have, yet you lived so far away. I always thought I could never form a closer bond with you because of the distance, and that thought has haunted me my whole life. Now, I'm so glad we caught up on the years that were taken from us as cousins.

I don't know how much you're aware of the fact that you really had a big influence in my development as a person, lowkey might've even been nothing to you, but the last time you came home to the Philippines with your whole family, you did my makeup, helped me with my costume, and I won "Best in Makeup" award at my schools party. I thought you were so cool that you knew so much about makeup and that really inspired me to start taking better care of myself and until now, I haven't really stopped learning from you.

Not exactly sure how you feel about the age you're turning to right now but I hope it treats you well, #manifesting there be more pages for you to color in, more booknooks to build, and more relaxing activities there are to discover that you can entertain yourself with.

Don't let the pressure of each year get to you, don't think that you have to rush into things just because your peers are doing so, and most importantly don't let yourself feel like you're behind on anything. Maybe you haven't even thought of any of these cause I feel like you're more like the type to be low cortisol but if ever in the future, at least you know I got your back.

Happy birthday! I hope you feel celebrated today, I'm sorry for not being able to do more. I promise in the future I'll be able to earn enough to fully treat you out.

With love,
Tasha ♡
